Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

The software. PathProbe runs locally on your computer. It does not include telemetry, analytics or tracking, and it does not send your network data, scan results or captures to us. Network tests you run (for example the internet check or WAN address lookup) contact the public hosts needed for that test. Pro license activation works offline.

Purchases. Payment data is processed by Paddle. Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns. Paddle collects your name, email, billing details and payment information under its own privacy policy. We receive your email, order details and license key so we can deliver and support your license. We never see your full card number.

Support email. If you email us, we keep the correspondence only as long as needed to help you and meet legal obligations.

Feedback form. If you use the feedback form, what you type (product, feedback type, optional version and system, your message, and your email address if you choose to give one) is sent to us as an email through our email provider, Resend, and handled like any other support email. The form does not use cookies or tracking. To block spam, the small Cloudflare Worker that receives the form briefly keeps a counter tied to a one-way, salted hash of your IP address; the hash changes daily and is deleted automatically within 24 hours. Your message and email address are not stored by the Worker or included in our site statistics.

Website. This site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic request data (such as IP address) for security. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-free, privacy-first measurement tool, to see how many people visit the site, which pages they view, and which sites they came from. We also count Buy-button clicks and campaign tags (for example, whether someone arrived from YouTube) with a small Cloudflare Worker that stores only product, event name, page path, and optional UTM labels — not IP addresses, names, or emails. Site download links for PathProbe and JackedIN go through a counter that records only anonymous per-file, per-day download counts; it excludes our own test traffic and known bots, and it does not store IP addresses, user agents, names, or emails. We do not use cookies, and we do not sell visitor data. See Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation for how that product collects data.

Your rights. You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold by emailing service@getpathprobe.com. We do not sell personal data.
