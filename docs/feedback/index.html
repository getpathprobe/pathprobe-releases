Leave feedback or report a problem

Found a bug, hit something confusing, or have an idea that would make PathProbe or JackedIN better? Tell us here. Your message goes straight to service@getpathprobe.com, and a person reads every one.

Please don't include license keys, passwords, API tokens or other secrets. Don't paste raw logs that contain your network details: for PathProbe, run pathprobe log share to make a scrubbed copy first, and for JackedIN, remove host names, addresses and anything you typed at a password prompt.

Which product?

Run pathprobe version or jackedin --version.

For a bug, say what you ran or clicked, what you expected, and what happened instead. 10 to 5,000 characters.
